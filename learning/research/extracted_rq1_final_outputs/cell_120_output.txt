PAPER-READY INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------
We directly tested whether the reasoning-oriented versus factual-recall separation in Qwen3-0.6B can be captured by one direction per layer. For each cross-validation fold, directions were constructed only from training examples, either as the mean-difference vector between task groups or as the normalized logistic-regression weight vector. Held-out examples were projected onto the resulting direction and evaluated as one-dimensional scores. The resulting layer-wise comparison should be interpreted against the full 1024-D linear probe and the existing surface-feature baselines. In particular, the strong TF-IDF baseline means that lexical and domain cues remain an important limitation, and this experiment does not establish a causal or intrinsic reasoning mechanism.
